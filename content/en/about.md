## About Me

I'm Ivan Claudio Fernandes, a technology and engineering leader with over 30 years of experience in the IT industry.

Throughout my career, I've worked across different areas of technology, from systems development and infrastructure to software architecture, DevOps, cloud-native engineering and technical leadership.

This broad experience has shaped the way I approach engineering challenges: technology decisions must consider not only implementation, but also operational reliability, maintainability, team productivity and business priorities.

## My Professional Journey

My career has included roles in systems analysis, application lifecycle management (ALM), QAOps and DevOps technical leadership, with professional experience at organizations such as Telefônica, B3, PrimeUp and Banco BV.

At Banco BV, I participated in digital transformation initiatives focused on DevOps practices and cloud-native technologies. Working with engineering teams, I contributed to initiatives involving CI/CD automation with Jenkins and Spinnaker, Kubernetes-based infrastructure, Terraform, Helm and observability solutions using Prometheus and Grafana.

These experiences strengthened my perspective on the importance of integrating software delivery, infrastructure automation and operational practices throughout the application lifecycle.

## Engineering and Technology Leadership

Currently, at Grupo Casas Bahia, I lead teams responsible for DevOps, Generative AI and IT Environment Management, while also working in a Technical Product Management capacity.

This combination of responsibilities allows me to work across engineering execution, platform capabilities, technology strategy and the alignment of technical initiatives with organizational priorities.

I see engineering leadership as a balance between enabling teams, making sound architectural decisions, managing operational risks and continuously improving how technology delivers value.

## My Approach to Technology

I believe successful technology transformation requires more than adopting new tools.

It involves establishing sustainable engineering practices, reducing unnecessary complexity, making systems more observable and creating the conditions for teams to work effectively.

My areas of interest and professional focus include DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering, cloud-native architecture, platform engineering, infrastructure automation and the responsible adoption of generative AI.

Knowledge sharing is also an important part of how I work. Throughout my career, I have conducted technical training sessions, workshops and practical learning activities related to software quality and cloud-native technologies.

## Sharing Knowledge and Experience

I use this space to share technical perspectives, reference architectures, practical experiments and lessons learned from working with complex technology environments.

My writing focuses on engineering decisions, the trade-offs behind them and the relationship between technical practices and organizational outcomes.

Whether discussing Kubernetes observability, DevOps maturity, engineering automation or AI-assisted workflows, my goal is to contribute practical insights that other technology professionals can evaluate, adapt and build upon.
